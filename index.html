StoryForge

StoryForge is a private, single-owner automation tool operated by Kadin Collins. It prepares short-form videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel (Elsewhere Unfolds) through the YouTube Data API, and reads that channel's statistics to improve future videos.

StoryForge is not offered to the public and does not access any YouTube account other than the owner's.
