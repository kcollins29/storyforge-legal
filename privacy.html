StoryForge Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

StoryForge is a private automation tool used only by its owner, Kadin Collins, to manage the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data StoryForge accesses

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, StoryForge uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set video metadata, and read channel and video information and analytics for the owner's channel. It does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to review their performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and service providers

OAuth tokens are held by Composio, the integration provider that brokers API calls on the owner's behalf. Video files and performance reports are stored on the owner's own servers.

Google and YouTube

StoryForge uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
